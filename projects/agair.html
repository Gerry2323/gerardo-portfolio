UAV TECHNOLOGY AND SUSTAINABLE AGRICULTURE

AgAir UAV System

Aerial Soil Analysis and Targeted Remediation

AgAir was developed by Team FALCONS for NASA’s Gateways to Blue Skies competition. The concept uses an unmanned aerial vehicle to analyze agricultural soil and deploy targeted remediation materials.

Project Overview

AgAir is a conceptual UAV-based agricultural system designed to help farmers identify soil problems and treat specific areas more efficiently.

The UAV would collect soil and environmental data using onboard sensors. The information would be used to locate areas requiring treatment, allowing remediation materials to be applied only where they are needed.

The Engineering Problem

Traditional agricultural soil testing can require manual sampling across large areas. This process may take significant time and can make it difficult to quickly identify changing soil conditions.

Applying fertilizer or treatment uniformly across an entire field can also waste material and increase costs. AgAir was designed to support a more targeted and data-driven approach.

Project Objectives

  • Develop a UAV concept for agricultural soil analysis.
  • Use sensors to identify variations in soil and crop conditions.
  • Create a system for mapping areas that require treatment.
  • Deploy targeted soil-remediation materials using pods.
  • Reduce unnecessary material use and operating costs.
  • Support more sustainable and precise agricultural practices.

How the System Works

  1. The UAV flies over a selected agricultural area.
  2. Onboard sensors collect soil, crop, and environmental data.
  3. The collected data is used to identify locations with possible soil deficiencies.
  4. A treatment plan is created for the affected areas.
  5. The UAV deploys remediation pods or materials at targeted locations.
  6. Additional flights can evaluate changes after treatment.

Major System Components

  • Unmanned aerial vehicle platform
  • Soil and environmental sensors
  • Navigation and flight-control system
  • Data collection and mapping system
  • Targeted remediation-pod deployment mechanism
  • Solar-powered operating base concept

Solar-Powered Base

The concept included a 10-kilowatt solar-powered base to support UAV charging and field operations. Using renewable power could reduce dependence on fuel and make the system more practical for remote agricultural locations.

Business Concept

The proposed concept used an agricultural service model. Farmers could receive soil analysis, mapping, and targeted remediation without purchasing and operating the complete UAV system themselves.

The preliminary service concept estimated a price of approximately $5,000 per hectare, depending on the mission, equipment, treatment materials, and operating requirements.

Potential Benefits

  • Faster collection of agricultural field data
  • More targeted use of remediation materials
  • Reduced waste and unnecessary treatment
  • Improved access to difficult or remote areas
  • Repeatable monitoring of changing field conditions
  • Support for sustainable precision agriculture
